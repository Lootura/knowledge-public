About

An entity-first map of what is happening

Lootura Entity Atlas is a public view of a living knowledge engine. It is built around companies, people, products, places, organizations, markets and topics, not around a reverse-chronological stream of articles.

The pipeline starts with news signals, groups related items, promotes high-confidence clusters into validated events, and lets those events update entities. News are temporary. The entity graph is the product.

The first public edition is English. It ingests selected feeds and processed Lootura outputs, stores metadata, removes duplicates, and looks for repeated evidence that the same event happened.

Validated events are immutable historical objects. They carry a title, summary, date, confidence, entities and source references. They are not the main public product; they are the mechanism that updates summaries, timelines, recent changes, related entities and gravity.

The public site stays intentionally small: a home page, an entity index, individual entity pages, this about page and product updates. Operational screens, source inspection and ingestion details belong elsewhere because they are useful for managing the system, but they add noise for readers, crawlers and AI agents.

The JSON is the source of truth. Astro renders HTML from that JSON, but the same entity files are designed to power future APIs, AI integrations, MCP servers or mobile clients.
